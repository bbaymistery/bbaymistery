# Hi there, I'm Elgun Ezmemmedov 👋

### Frontend Engineer · React, Next.js & TypeScript

🌍 **Portfolio:** [elgun.vercel.app](https://elgun.vercel.app/) · 📍 Baku, Azerbaijan (UTC+4) · Open to remote

Frontend Engineer with **5+ years of experience** building production web applications with React, Next.js and TypeScript. For four years, I worked remotely for **Airport Pickups London ([aplcars.com](https://aplcars.com))**, where I grew from Junior Next.js Developer to Frontend Engineer and owned the booking and payment flows of an international airport-transfer platform.

I'm now growing my full-stack skills with **Node.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL and AWS**.

### 🚀 Highlights

* **Manage Booking widget:** One React widget used across **7 company websites**. Customers view, edit, pay for and cancel their bookings themselves, with live re-quoting and Stripe surcharge payments.
* **Live flight tracking:** Pickup times are recalculated from actual and estimated landing data in the airport's time zone, so drivers and passengers stay in sync during delays.
* **Booking & payments:** Owned the 5-step booking funnel (quote → vehicle → transfer details → payment → confirmation) and expanded checkout to **Stripe, PayPal and American Express**.
* **Performance & SEO:** Raised Lighthouse scores from **77 to 90+** on a 7-language site with SSR/SSG/ISR, code splitting and lazy loading. Added technical SEO (JSON-LD, canonical URLs) and GEO for AI-driven search.
* **AI / MCP:** Built MCP integrations so Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini can fetch real transfer quotes → [ai.airport-pickups-london.com](https://ai.airport-pickups-london.com/)
* **Community:** Founder & Organizer of **Speak Socially** [![Instagram](https://img.shields.io/badge/Instagram-E4405F?style=flat&logo=instagram&logoColor=white)](https://www.instagram.com/speak__socially/), a language-exchange and cultural community with **200+ events** hosted.
